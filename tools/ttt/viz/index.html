Notation

Each state is a group of m transitions, one per symbol: write symbol, move (P = one cell the same way as the last move, T = turn around and move one cell), next state. --- or a target beyond the last state (e.g. Z) halts; the halting step counts as a step. The head starts in state A, facing right, on a blank tape. The toot transition is a (state, symbol) pair such as A4; a toot on the halting transition scores the halting step.
